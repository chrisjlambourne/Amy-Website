Welcome

Hi, I'm Amy. I originally qualified as a Beauty Therapist in 2004, and since then I've found my true passion is giving people beautiful nails.

Whether it's an elegant french polish for a wedding, a glam glittery look for a big night out, or an everyday beautiful pastel shade, I am here to help. I only work with industry-leading products to ensure your nails stay healthy and beautiful.

My Philosophy

I pride myself on ensuring every visit to the salon is an enjoyable one. When your treatments are complete every client should leave feeling great and looking forward to their next visit.

Natural nail health is at the heart of what I do, cleanliness in the salon is a big priority, as is aftercare following your visit. I am fully insured and always keep my training up to date.

Manicures

Using CND Shellac, Vinylux or Artistic for excellent adhesion and a clean, long-lasting finish.

Pedicures

Relaxing foot massages, exfoliation, and essential cuticle maintenance for beautiful, healthy feet and nails.

Nail Art

A selection of glitters, foils, chrome, ombre, catseye and stamping to create your unique individual style.
