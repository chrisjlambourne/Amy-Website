Nail Art Guide

Nail art is a beautiful way to add personality, detail and creativity to your manicure. Every design takes a different amount of time, skill and precision, so nail art is priced in tiers to keep things simple, fair and transparent.

If you have a specific design in mind, you're always welcome to send inspiration photos before your appointment so I can advise on the most suitable tier and provide an accurate quote.

Tier One

Simple Art

from £5

Tier 1 covers simpler nail art that adds a stylish finishing touch without taking too much time to create. This includes classic French tips, ombré, glitter fades, foils, and simple or minimal designs.

It also includes smaller accent designs such as animal print or blooming gel on up to four nails.

This tier is ideal if you want something elegant, subtle and low-maintenance, while still giving your nails a little extra detail.

Included in this tier

  • Classic French tips (full set)
  • Ombré & colour fades
  • Glitter fades
  • Foils
  • Simple & minimal designs
  • Animal print (up to 4 nails)
  • Blooming gel (up to 4 nails)

Tier Two

Medium Art

from £8

Tier 2 is for designs that take a bit more time, precision and creativity. This includes detailed or double-lined French, single-coloured chrome, stamping on four or more nails, nail crystals, mix-and-match sets, and full sets of animal print or blooming gel designs.

This tier is perfect if you want your nails to stand out a little more and include more detailed design work across the set.

Included in this tier

  • Detailed or double-lined French
  • Single-colour chrome
  • Multiple swirls
  • Stamping on 4 or more nails
  • Nail crystals
  • Mix & match sets
  • Animal print (full set)
  • Blooming gel (full set)

Tier Three

Detailed Art

from £12

Tier 3 is for more advanced, time-consuming nail art that requires a higher level of detail and artistry. This includes hand-painted designs, detailed florals, 3D art, full intricate sets, and more elaborate chrome designs.

Because this tier is based on complexity rather than simply the number of nails involved, the final price may vary depending on the design. More detailed or time-consuming artwork may be priced higher.

Included in this tier

  • Hand-painted designs
  • Detailed florals
  • 3D art
  • Full intricate sets
  • Elaborate chrome designs
  • Bespoke & custom artwork

Important Information

Nail art pricing is based on design complexity, skill level and the time required to complete the look, rather than just the number of nails involved. Some designs may fall between tiers or require a custom quote.

If you're unsure which tier your chosen design falls into, please send a photo in advance and I'll be happy to advise.
