Aftercare Advice

Protecting your natural nails

Thank you for choosing me for your natural nail treatment. Regardelss of which brand we've used, following this advice is essential to keep your nails healthy and your colour lasting as long as possible.

Jewels, Not Tools

Use the pads of your fingers or a utensil to open cans and parcels. A sudden knock can cause product to chip or lift.

Oil, Oil, Oil

The secret to longevity. Apply CND™ SolarOil™ twice daily to keep the natural nail flexible and hydrated and to prevent dry crusty skin around your nails.

Wear Gloves

Always wear rubber gloves when cleaning or gardening. Chemicals and water can cause lifting, dullness, chips & peeling.

Product-Specific Care

CND™ Shellac™ & Luxe™

  • Sun & Swim: Chlorine and suntan lotions (especially those containing DEET) can weaken Shellac. Wash hands thoroughly after exposure.
  • Drying: Avoid intense heat (saunas, steam rooms) for the first 24 hours to allow the bond to settle.
  • Maintenance: Designed to last 14+ days. Please book removal in advance to avoid picking.

Nail (Glitters & Foils)

  • Top Coat Protection: Your art is sealed within the gel. If you notice snagging, contact me immediately. Do not pick the edges!
  • Avoid Staining: Pigmented items (new denim, hair dye, spices) can stain light glitters. Wash hands immediately after exposure.

IBX™ Strengthening

  • Invisible Shield: IBX fuses nail layers together inside the nail. You won't see it, but it's there.
  • Continuous Care: It grows out with your nail. Daily oiling is crucial to keep the plate tough yet flexible.
  • Follow-up: Stick to the 2–3 week schedule for best results.

Artistic Colour Gloss

  • High Shine: Avoid hand creams with Lanolin or Mineral Oil as they can dull the shine.
  • Sanitizers: Excessive alcohol gel use can dry out the product. Re-hydrate with oil afterwards.

Removal & Emergency Tips

🚫 NEVER Pick or Peel: Picking off your coating will peel away layers of your natural nail, leaving them thin, weak, and sensitive.


💅 Professional Removal: Please return to the salon for professional removal. I use safe, gentle methods to protect your nail integrity.


🆘 Emergency Fix: If a nail chips or lifts, do not glue it down. This can trap bacteria. Contact me for a repair, or carefully file the rough edge until you can see me.

Regular maintenance is key. I recommend booking every 2–3 weeks.
