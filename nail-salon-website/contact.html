The Salon

The salon is a welcoming space purpose-built within our home that offers a one-to-one peaceful environment designed for comfort and relaxation.

Free parking is provided on our private driveway and complimentary hot and cold drinks will always be available for your refreshment. I look forward to welcoming you soon.

Booking Policy & Etiquette

Cancellations

I kindly request at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. This allows me to offer the appointment to another client.

Please note: Late cancellations or missed appointments will incur a £10 fee.

Health & Wellness

For the safety of myself and other clients, please do not attend if you or anyone in your household has experienced sickness within the last 48 hours.

I am happy to reschedule your appointment for when you are feeling better.

Salon Etiquette

  • Arrivals: Please arrive promptly. Arriving too early may interrupt the previous client's relaxation, while arriving late may reduce your treatment time.
  • Payments: Payment is due at the end of your treatment via Cash, Bank Transfer or Contactless.
