Our Premium Partners

Quality ingredients for healthy, beautiful nails

We believe that beautiful nails start with healthy nails. That’s why we have carefully selected the industry’s most respected brands to ensure every treatment provides longevity, shine, and care.

Strengthening & Repair

IBX System

+

The IBX System is a revolutionary treatment for weak, peeling, or damaged nails. Unlike traditional hardeners that just sit on top of the nail, IBX uses heat and light to penetrate the nail plate and fuse the upper layers together. It strengthens your natural nail from the inside out, acting as a protective shield under Gel Polish or as a powerful standalone treatment to encourage growth.

Gel & Colour Systems

CND™ Shellac™

+

Known globally as "The Original Power Polish," CND Shellac combines the ease of polish with the permanence of gel. It offers 14+ days of high-performance wear, stunning crystal shine, and zero dry time. Best of all, it requires no roughing of the natural nail, keeping your nails healthy and strong.

Artistic Colour Gloss

+

For those who love vibrant, edgy, and distinct shades, we use Artistic Colour Gloss. This soak-off gel formula is designed to last up to 21 days without peeling or chipping. It glides on beautifully and cures in seconds, leaving you with a high-gloss finish that stays flawless until your next appointment.

CND™ Luxe

+

The premium upgrade. CND Luxe is designed for clients who want the very best in speed and care. Offering the same high-gloss shine you love, this luxury formula is designed for faster application and, crucially, gentle removal in as little as 60 seconds—saving you time while pampering your nails.

Specialized Foot Care

Footlogix®

+

We trust Footlogix for truly transformational results. As the world’s first and only "Pediceutical" foot care line, it bridges the gap between a relaxing pedicure and medicinal therapy. Their unique mousse-based formulas absorb instantly into the skin without leaving a greasy residue, effectively treating dry heels and skin conditions to leave your feet baby-soft.

Spa & Relaxation

CND™ Spa

+

Elevate your manicure or pedicure into a sensory journey. The CND Spa collection uses botanical ingredients to exfoliate, smooth, and hydrate the skin. Whether it’s the brightening glow of citrus or the soothing calm of cucumber, these treatments provide a moment of pure indulgence.

Nail Art & Aftercare

Lecenté™ Nail Art

+

Express your personality with our extensive Lecenté collection. From subtle shimmers and holographics to bold foils and chunky glitters, Lecenté offers the highest quality, cosmetic-grade nail art. It is safe, long-lasting, and the perfect way to add a bespoke sparkle to your manicure.

CND™ SolarOil™

+

Often called a "miracle in a bottle," this award-winning cuticle oil is an essential part of your nail care routine. Infused with jojoba oil, sweet almond oil, and Vitamin E, it penetrates deep into the nail plate to keep natural nails and enhancements tough, flexible, and hydrated.
